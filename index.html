How this works (nothing you type leaves your browser)
  1. On first visit your browser downloads a small AI model, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (about 23 MB), via Transformers.js. After that it's cached.
  2. Ahead of time, every post was split into its sections, and each section was turned into a list of 384 numbers (an embedding). Sections about similar ideas get similar numbers. Those numbers ship as one small file.
  3. Your question becomes 384 numbers too, right here in your browser. Every section is scored against it, and each post's best section wins. That's the whole "vector database": an array and a loop.
  4. That's why each result can tell you which section of the post answers you.
  5. New posts are picked up from my Medium RSS feed by a scheduled GitHub Action. No server involved.
